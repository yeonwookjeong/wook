## 1단계: 시안 3개 비교 (첫 디자인)

```
Use the attached character (a Joseon-era official called "Hundo") ONLY as a style reference: copy his exact art style — semi-chibi Korean webtoon proportions, clean dark brown line art, soft cel shading, warm skin tones, plain white background, bust-up framing.

Draw a DIFFERENT character from the same world: a young Joseon street storyteller (jeongisu, 傳奇叟) who reads novels aloud to crowds in Jongno, Seoul, in the late 1700s.

Show 3 design variations side by side in one image, same framing as the reference.

The storyteller must look clearly different from Hundo:
- Hat: a traditional Korean gat — wide ROUND brim, semi-transparent black horsehair, worn slightly tilted. NOT the winged official hat (bokdu).
- Clothes: white / ivory dopo robe with an indigo-blue (jjok) sash cord. NO green, NO gold star or constellation patterns.
- Props: holding an open hanji-bound Korean novel (handwritten hangul pages), a string of brass coins (yeopjeon) tied at the waist. NO folding fan.
- Face: round bright eyes, big playful grin, slightly messy bangs, expressive eyebrows — a charming, slightly mischievous entertainer, the opposite of Hundo's calm cool smirk.
- Age: looks early 20s.

No text, no letters, no watermark anywhere in the image.
```
